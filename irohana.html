Why it looks like this

The palette is your kanji app's, unchanged. It was already blossom pink, which is a lucky match for the name — so the kanji half barely changes, and the vocabulary half moves across to meet it.

Two fonts only. Shippori Mincho for Japanese and headings, Noto Sans JP for everything else — the same pair the vocabulary app already uses.

Three text sizes only: 12, 14, 16. The big Japanese word on a flashcard and the logo are artwork, not interface text, so they sit outside that scale on purpose.

The switch lives in the top bar. Inside Vocabulary you see "→ Kanji", and the reverse — one tap between the two halves, no going home first.

Separate rankings, one account. Two tabs on one leaderboard, reading two score columns on the same profile.
