いろはこ — “the iroha box”. The two strokes of the ha are petals: one the blue of the goi flower, one the pink of the kanji flower. The name is tinted the same way, blue half and pink half.

Why these two colours

They are not new colours. The blue is lifted from the goi flower (#7c83ff) and the pink from the kanji flower (#ff8fbb) — the same two logos that already sit in the corner of both sites. So the name carries the two halves of the project inside it.

One thing to check: at 16px the two petals start to merge into one shape. That is fine in a sentence, but for a browser-tab icon I would use a single flower rather than this wordmark.
